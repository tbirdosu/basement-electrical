Entering Advanced Programming Mode

Do this on the MACL-153M (the primary), not the MA-R:

  1. Pull the FASST tab (bottom of the switch) out, wait 5 seconds.
  2. Hold the tap button, push the FASST tab back in while still holding, keep holding ~5 seconds.
  3. Release when the bottom indicator light starts blinking slowly.
  4. Use raise/lower to pick an option, tap to enter it.

No activity for a minute exits automatically. To save and exit immediately, hold the tap button until the indicator light stops blinking. Full factory reset uses the same entry sequence but hold the tap button for 15 seconds instead of 5.

Good to know
Fade ON/OFF times only apply when switching from the MACL-153M or MA-R. If a plain mechanical 3-way switch is also in the circuit, toggling from that ignores the fade setting entirely.

Turning Off Night Light Mode

Enter option 5, press raise/lower to turn the indicator glow off, tap to save. This can also improve compatibility with LED/CFL bulbs that don't fully extinguish on some dimmers.

Fixing Low-End LED Flicker

Option 6 (low-end trim) is the one to use. Shortcut: hold tap + lower together until an LED blinks, adjust with raise/lower until the bulb is steady at its lowest setting, tap to save.

Smart Home / App Control

The MACL-153M has no radio — the advanced programming mode described above is the entire "settings" interface. There's no upgrade path or add-on module; getting app or voice control means replacing both boxes:

  • Caséta dimmer (PD-6WCL) or Diva Smart Dimmer (DVRF-6L) — the Diva looks closer to a standard paddle. Both require the Lutron Smart Hub.
  • The MA-R companion is replaced with a Pico remote — Caséta doesn't support MA-R at all. The smart dimmer goes in the primary box; the companion box gets its wires capped and a Pico mounted over it.
  • Sunnata touch dimmers are also available in RF versions if you'd prefer a different paddle style.
⚠ If the fixture is an integrated smart light
Don't put an integrated-LED smart fixture (its own driver + Wi-Fi radio, e.g. Hubspace) on any dimmer. It needs constant full line voltage — dimming it externally causes flicker, buzzing, or a fixture that won't power up. Pull both switches and replace with two plain mechanical 3-way switches left permanently on. Brightness, scheduling and color move into the fixture's own app.

Your Hardware

Dimmer
Leviton DSL01
No-neutral (2-wire) slide dimmer. Two installed — one at each end of a 3-way run.
Fixtures
3× LED disk lights (generic, Amazon)
Unbranded flush-mount disks. Not on Leviton's dimmer compatibility list — nobody's tested them against it.

Root Cause

Diagnosis
Two dimmers wired into one 3-way circuit. A 3-way with dimming can only have one dimmer — the other location must be a plain mechanical 3-way switch.

Both DSL01s are no-neutral, phase-cut dimmers that steal operating power through the load and time their triac firing off the AC waveform. With two in series, each one chops the waveform the other is trying to read for its zero-cross timing — neither can hold a stable firing point. That produces flicker across the entire dimming range. Adding two more fixtures tripled the inrush current spike at each firing, pushing it over the edge.

The Fix

Keep one DSL01 as the dimmer. Replace the dimmer at the other end with a plain 3-way switch:

  • Leviton 5603-2W — standard white Decora paddle
  • Leviton 5603-2AW — same, antimicrobial coating
  • Leviton 5603-2WS — same, no coating

All are 15A, 120V, Decora rocker — matches the DSL01 paddle you're keeping.

Wiring the Replacement

Before you start
Kill the breaker and verify dead with a tester. Label the wire on the old dimmer's common/black terminal before disconnecting — that's the one wire that matters for correct reconnection.
Common
Wire from old dimmer's black/common terminal → new switch's dark/common screw.
Travelers
The two remaining wires → the two brass screws, either order.
Ground
Ground wire → green screw.

If Flicker Persists After the Swap

  1. Confirm same batch: mixed LED drivers beat against each other. Make sure all three disks are the identical model/batch.
  2. Diagnostic test: temporarily clip an incandescent or halogen load onto the circuit. Smooth dimming with that load confirms the dimmer's fine and the drivers are the weak link.
  3. Better dimmer: Lutron Diva LED+ (DVCL-153P) handles bad driver inrush noticeably better than the DSL01, with a wider low-end trim range. Drop-in replacement in the same 3-way.
  4. Last resort — dummy load: Lutron LUT-MLC flicker-eliminator module, wired in parallel at one fixture, smooths out driver-timing fights the dimmer can't otherwise fix.

"Dimmable" on a generic listing usually just means the fixture won't be damaged on a dimmer — not that it dims smoothly. If the swap doesn't fully resolve it, new fixtures from Leviton's tested-compatible list (Halo, Lithonia, Feit) are the more reliable long-term fix.
